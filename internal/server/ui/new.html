Connections

Connections are defined once, on the connections page, and chosen here. With a registry connection, the form offers the deploy artifacts and tags of its user. With a Cloudflare connection, the app gets a tunnel of its own in that account and a DNS record in its zone. Without one, it stays inside the cluster, or gets a quick tunnel: a random address that needs no account and no domain, but changes whenever the tunnel restarts, and is meant for trying things out.

Domain

With a domain, the app answers at <app>{{.Status.Settings.HostSuffix}}.<domain>; without one, at <app>.shelf.internal inside the cluster. A Cloudflare connection needs a domain: a zone of its account.
